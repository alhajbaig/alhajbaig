## 👨‍💻 The Man, The Myth, The Model Trainer

I'm a CS engineering undergrad hailing from **Nagpur, India**. When I'm not trying to figure out why my neural network thinks a stop sign is a toaster, I'm working across the full ML spectrum. 

My philosophy is simple: **the best way to understand AI is to build with it.** Every project I ship teaches me what no course can. Every failure tells me something true. I fuel my commits with unhealthy amounts of chicken shawarma, and when the screen fatigue hits, you'll usually find me lifting weights at the gym to de-bug my brain.
